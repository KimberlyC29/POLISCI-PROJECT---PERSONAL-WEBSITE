Second-year undergraduate studying Science, Technology, and Society

Research Interests

I study the structures that shape technology: the laws, institutions, economic incentives, and social norms that influence what gets built and who it serves. I am especially interested in the political implications of AI around the world, including how different countries regulate it, who holds power over it, and who bears its risks.

Project Idea

My project will look at the use of AI in law firms. Legal work depends on judgment, confidentiality, and accountability, so it is a useful place to ask how AI changes professional practice.

I plan to ask how firms decide which AI tools to adopt, how the work of junior associates and paralegals changes, and who is responsible when an AI tool makes a mistake. I also want to compare how professional rules and regulations shape adoption in different places. I expect to draw on interviews, firm and bar association policies, and published case studies.
